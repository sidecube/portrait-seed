# Illustration provenance

`images/overview.png` was commissioned for this package using the built-in
OpenAI Imagegen tool on 5 November 2026. No visitor photograph or reference
portrait was supplied. It is a conceptual illustration, not an output obtained
by running the camera-to-portrait code. The replacement follows the installation's
visual language: a black background, grayscale activation-like patterns, white
pixel fragments and photographic fictional portraits. These patterns are visual
metaphors, not captured measurements. Original raster output is included unchanged.

Prompt:

> Create a new wide 16:9 editorial cover image for the open-source project Portrait Seed, visually in the style of a minimal black-screen contemporary AI portrait installation. This replaces an unwanted beige vintage collage. Absolute black #080808 background, restrained white and grey pixels and a tiny pale sage accent, clean precise computer-rendered art, no paper, no illustrations in pencil, no vintage motifs, no neon, no holographic sci-fi, no floating glass, no gradients. Composition: on the left third a sparse square field of tiny white activation dots and three small grayscale neural feature-map mosaics in a disciplined grid. In the centre a narrow quiet transition of structured white typographic-like pixel fragments dissolving toward the right, suggesting visual features become language. On the right half, a large highly realistic photographic studio head-and-shoulders portrait of a fictional adult person facing straight toward camera, cropped in a simple upright rectangle, warm neutral photographic background only inside the portrait, unembellished natural skin and calm expression. Two much smaller frontal photographic portrait thumbnails aligned as a neat strip below or beside the large portrait indicate a growing collection. Real photography aesthetic, high-end restrained exhibition documentation, clear contrast between dark computational panels and photographic portrait. Large calm black negative space, no arrows, no connecting flourishes, no icons, no camera drawing. NO TEXT AT ALL, no lettering, no labels, no title, no watermark. Professional museum digital installation aesthetic, careful alignment, simple flat composition, crisp pixels and natural portrait detail. Entirely fictional people, no real visitor photo reference.

`images/data-flow.svg` and `images/lifecycle.svg` are authored vector diagrams
based on the packaged code. They contain explicit boundaries and retention labels
and can be edited as text. They contain no external resources or scripts.
